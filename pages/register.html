What to do next
  1. Give the user their username and initial password through a private channel, such as in person or by phone. Don't send both in the same email or chat message.

How registration works
  • Only administrators create accounts. There is no public sign-up page.
  • Users sign in with their username, not their email address.
  • Usernames and email addresses must be unique. If one is already taken, you'll see a message next to that field.
  • You set the initial password. CMS-PPA can't display it after the account is created, so make sure you can pass it on to the user.
